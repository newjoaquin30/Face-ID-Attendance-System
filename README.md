# FACE ID ATTENDANCE SYSTEM (Recreation since research defense)

# Face-ID-Attendance-System
- A system that scans for the student's name and face and automatically records the attendance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# How it works?
- A student will login to their account and will be navigated to the dashboard. Once navigated, they will go to
their perspective subjects to take their attendance. And after they are at their subjects, they will click on the attendance hyperlink and will bring them to the attendance page where the camera frame is displayed. Once done, they will be marked as of today's attendance

# Features inside the Face ID
- Submit Attendance: submitting the attendance once the student has filled up his/her name.
- Scan Face: He/she will click on the button to scan his/her face and a popup alert will appear on the top.
- Clear Attendance: A student will be able to clear his/her attendance if he/she misspelled his/her name.

# Features missing in the Face ID System (Will be added soon)
